# Governance

This project is maintained under the Itonami Occupation Blueprint framework. Decisions regarding actor design, governor rules, and occupational scope are guided by the ISCO-08 unit group 2146 specification and professional engineering standards for mining and metallurgical practice.

## Core Principles

1. **Domain Scope Integrity** — The actor operates strictly within the occupational domain defined for Mining Engineers, Metallurgists and Related Professionals. Licensed engineer authority is never violated.

2. **Human-in-the-Loop** — All safety-critical and high-risk operations escalate to human review. The actor never suppresses flags or makes autonomous decisions on flagged conditions.

3. **Auditability** — All proposals, verdicts, and records are logged to an append-only ledger. No record can be deleted or modified after commitment.

4. **Transparency** — Governor rules and operational boundaries are publicly documented. No hidden limitations or silent failures.
